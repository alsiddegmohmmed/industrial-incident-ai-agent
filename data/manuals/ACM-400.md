# ACM-400 Industrial Motor Manual

## Thermal operating limits

Normal motor winding temperature is below 80 degrees Celsius. A reading above
80 degrees Celsius is a warning and a reading above 90 degrees Celsius is a
critical condition. When temperature is critical, reduce the load if the
operating procedure permits it and arrange an inspection before returning the
motor to unrestricted service.

## Vibration indicators

Vibration above 4.5 mm/s requires investigation. Vibration above 7.1 mm/s is a
critical alarm threshold. Rising vibration together with a rising temperature
is consistent with bearing, alignment, or lubrication problems, but the manual
does not treat any one cause as confirmed without a physical inspection.

## Load and temperature interpretation

Sustained load above 90 percent can increase temperature. If load is below 90
percent while temperature and vibration are high, prioritize mechanical checks
over an overload explanation. Compare the current state with recent telemetry
before selecting a maintenance action.

## Inspection guidance

Do not reset a critical alarm as a substitute for inspection. Record the current
temperature, vibration, load, and alert code. A qualified technician should
inspect bearings, coupling alignment, and lubrication condition before a repair
or restart decision.
