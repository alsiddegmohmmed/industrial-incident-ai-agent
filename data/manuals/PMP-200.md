# PMP-200 Pump Manual

## Telemetry availability

The pump controller reports temperature, vibration, speed, and load telemetry.
When all measurements are unavailable and the device status is unknown, treat
the condition as a telemetry or communication fault rather than assuming the
pump is stopped or healthy.

## Communication checks

Confirm the controller power and network link, inspect the telemetry gateway
status, and check the most recent communication alert. Do not infer physical
measurements while the telemetry path is unavailable.

## Return to service

After communication is restored, verify that new samples have current
timestamps and plausible values. Record the outage and compare the recovered
signals with the pump's normal operating range before clearing the alert.
